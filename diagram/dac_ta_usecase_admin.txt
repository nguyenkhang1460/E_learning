=================================================================
BANG 1. DAC TA USE CASE DANG NHAP
Use case: Dang nhap
Tac nhan: Quan tri vien (Admin)
Mo ta:
Use case nay cho phep Admin dang nhap vao he thong de truy cap cac chuc nang quan tri.
He thong kiem tra thong tin tai khoan va phan quyen truoc khi cho phep su dung.
Luong su kien chinh:
1. Admin mo trang dang nhap.
2. Admin nhap ten dang nhap va mat khau.
3. He thong kiem tra thong tin tai khoan trong CSDL.
4. He thong xac nhan quyen Admin.
5. He thong chuyen den trang admin_dashboard va ket thuc use case.

=================================================================
BANG 2. DAC TA USE CASE DOI MAT KHAU
Use case: Doi mat khau
Tac nhan: Quan tri vien (Admin)
Mo ta:
Use case nay cho phep Admin thay doi mat khau de tang bao mat tai khoan.
He thong chi cap nhat khi mat khau hien tai hop le va mat khau moi dat yeu cau.
Luong su kien chinh:
1. Admin chon chuc nang doi mat khau.
2. He thong hien thi form doi mat khau.
3. Admin nhap mat khau cu, mat khau moi va xac nhan mat khau moi.
4. He thong kiem tra tinh hop le du lieu.
5. He thong cap nhat mat khau va thong bao thanh cong.

=================================================================
BANG 3. DAC TA USE CASE DANG XUAT
Use case: Dang xuat
Tac nhan: Quan tri vien (Admin)
Mo ta:
Use case nay cho phep Admin ket thuc phien lam viec an toan.
He thong huy session hien tai va chuyen ve trang dang nhap.
Luong su kien chinh:
1. Admin nhan nut Dang xuat.
2. He thong ghi nhan yeu cau dang xuat.
3. He thong huy session dang nhap.
4. He thong dieu huong ve trang dang nhap.
5. Use case ket thuc.

BANG 4. DAC TA USE CASE TAO LOP HOC MOI
Use case: Tao lop hoc moi
Tac nhan: Quan tri vien (Admin)
Mo ta:
Use case nay cho phep Admin tao mot lop hoc moi trong he thong.
He thong luu thong tin lop hoc va gan Admin la nguoi quan ly.
Luong su kien chinh:
1. Admin vao chuc nang tao lop hoc.
2. He thong hien thi form tao lop.
3. Admin nhap thong tin lop (ten lop, mo ta, ma lop, ...).
4. He thong kiem tra du lieu bat buoc va tinh duy nhat cua ma lop.
5. He thong tao lop hoc moi trong CSDL va thong bao thanh cong.

=================================================================

Use case: Chinh sua lop hoc
Tac nhan: Quan tri vien (Admin)

Luong su kien chinh:

2. Admin chon mot lop can chinh sua.
3. He thong hien thi thong tin lop hien tai.
4. Admin cap nhat cac truong thong tin can thiet.
5. He thong luu thay doi va thong bao thanh cong.

BANG 6. DAC TA USE CASE XOA LOP HOC
Use case: Xoa lop hoc
Tac nhan: Quan tri vien (Admin)
Mo ta:
Use case nay cho phep Admin xoa mot lop hoc khong con su dung.
He thong yeu cau xac nhan truoc khi xoa de tranh mat du lieu ngoai y muon.
Luong su kien chinh:

2. Admin chon lop hoc can xoa.
3. He thong hien thi canh bao xac nhan xoa.

5. He thong xoa du lieu lien quan theo quy tac va thong bao ket qua.

BANG 7. DAC TA USE CASE XEM/QUAN LY LOP HOC
Use case: Xem/Quan ly lop hoc
Tac nhan: Quan tri vien (Admin)
Mo ta:
Use case nay cho phep Admin xem danh sach, tra cuu va thao tac tren cac lop hoc.
Use case la diem bat dau cho cac nghiep vu tao/sua/xoa/luu tru lop.
Luong su kien chinh:
1. Admin mo trang quan ly lop hoc.
2. He thong tai danh sach lop hoc cua Admin.
3. Admin loc, tim kiem hoac chon mot lop cu the.
4. He thong hien thi chi tiet lop hoc va cac thao tac kha dung.
5. Admin tiep tuc voi use case con (sua, xoa, luu tru...) hoac ket thuc.

4. Admin xac nhan luu tru lop hoc.
5. He thong cap nhat trang thai lop hoc sang luu tru va thong bao thanh cong.
